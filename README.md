## 👋 About Me

I'm an **AI/LLM and full-stack engineer** with 5+ years of shipping production systems. I'm the sole engineer behind a production **RAG pipeline**, an **MCP-based agent**, and a **WhatsApp AI assistant**, and I've led teams building **adversarial coding evaluations** that train LLMs toward more reliable outcomes.

Before that, I scaled secure sign-in for a large consumer base, upgraded legacy Django stacks, and built GraphQL APIs focused on performance and security. I'm also a **CPA** with a background in financial accounting and bookkeeping, plus a published author on **mathematical modelling for policy making**.

>  **What makes me different:** I can design it, build it end to end, deploy it, *and* understand the financial logic behind it.
