## 👋 About Me

I am an **AI/LLM and Systems Engineer** with 5+ years of experience designing, shipping, and maintaining production-grade infrastructure. My work spans **RAG pipelines**, **MCP-based agents**, **WhatsApp AI integrations**, and **adversarial LLM evaluation benchmarks** (SWE-bench style).
